Premium Nail Cones

Normal & Bridal Cones

Before Care (Pre-Application)

  • Hands should be completely clean and dry before starting.
  • Avoid any oil, lotion, cream, or soap residue on the skin area.
  • Do not wax or bleach the application area recently (Complete this 2 days before).
  • Good natural body heat helps yield an intensely rich stain profile.
  • Overnight paste application gives the finest and longest-lasting results.

After Care (Post-Removal)

  • Keep henna paste on the skin for a minimum of 6 to 8 hours.
  • Remove dry paste completely without using any water.
  • Soak with Aftercare oil (coconut, clove, almond, or olive oil) and scrape gently using a tissue or dry cloth.
  • Strictly avoid water, harsh soaps, and hand sanitizers for 12 hours.
  • Stain darkens naturally over a window of 24 to 48 hours.
  • Warmth helps develop depth—avoid direct air conditioning immediately after removal.

Cone Storage Instructions

  • Store cones inside your deep freezer immediately upon arrival.
  • Our premium organic paste retains high stain properties for up to 2-3 months when frozen properly.
  • Thaw individual units by keeping them at room temperature for 20-30 minutes prior to execution.
  • Do not refreeze a cone structurally once it has been completely thawed.
  • If maintained purely at room temperature, consume the paste within 24 hours.
