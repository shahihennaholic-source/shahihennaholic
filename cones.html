Cone Storage Instructions

  • Store cones in freezer immediately after receiving.
  • Cones remain fresh for up to 2-3 months in proper freezing conditions.
  • Keep outside for 20-30 minutes before use.
  • Do not refreeze after thawing.
  • Use within 24 hours if stored at room temperature.
  • Proper storage improves stain quality and freshness.

Before Care (Before Applying Henna)

  • Hands should be clean & dry
  • Avoid oil / lotion / cream / soap residue
  • Do not wax or bleach the area recently (Do 2 days before)
  • Good body heat gives better stain
  • Overnight application gives best results

After Care (After Removing Henna)

  • Keep henna for minimum 6–8 hours
  • Remove paste without using water
  • Soak with Aftercare oil (coconut / clove / almond / olive oil) and scrape gently using tissue or dry cloth
  • Avoid water, soap & sanitizer for 12 hours
  • Stain darkens naturally in 24–48 hours
  • Heat helps stain – avoid AC immediately
