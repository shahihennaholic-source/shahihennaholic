🧊 Cone Storage Instructions

Follow these steps to preserve maximum colour potency and freshness

📦

Freeze immediately upon receiving your order

🗓️

Stays fresh & potent for 2–3 months when frozen

⏱️

Thaw at room temperature for 20–30 mins before use

🚫

Never refreeze a cone once fully thawed

🌈

Proper storage ensures vibrant, rich colour output

✦ Application Care ✦

Before & After Care Guide

🌿

Before Care

Prepare your skin for the deepest stain

Ensure skin area is completely clean and dry before application

Avoid applying oil, lotion, cream, or any soap residue

Do not wax or bleach the area within 2 days of application

Good natural body heat helps the stain develop beautifully

Leaving henna on overnight yields the deepest possible colour

✨

After Care

Maximise your stain's depth and longevity

Keep the henna design on for a minimum of 6–8 hours

Scrape paste away gently — do not use water for removal

Soak with coconut or clove oil to loosen dry crust flakes

Avoid water, liquid soaps, or sanitisers for at least 12 hours

The stain darkens naturally and beautifully over 24–48 hours
